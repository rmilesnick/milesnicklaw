About Rob Milesnick

Three vantage points. One practice.

Rob has represented employees, prosecuted fraud for the federal government, defended employers, and mediated hundreds of disputes. That's not a resume — it's a way of seeing cases that few attorneys have.

Background

Rob Milesnick

Rob is a former Assistant U.S. Attorney and a Washington and Oregon employment law litigator with a focus on whistleblower, civil rights and complex litigation alleging gender-based discrimination, sexual harassment, retaliation and hostile work environment claims.

In addition to his litigation experience helping employees and workers, Rob has been a certified mediator for the past 23 years, and in addition to his work as an AUSA, he served a collateral duty appointment to solve EEO disputes as mediator with the Executive Office of U.S. Attorneys. He is a court approved mediator for the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon and the Western District of Washington, serves on the Board of Directors of the Oregon Mediation Association and served as an elected member of the Board of Governors for the Oregon State Bar from 2020–2024.

Rob received his Bachelor of Arts, cum laude in English and Psychology from Virginia Commonwealth University in 1999 and his Juris Doctor from Syracuse University College of Law in 2002, where he was a member of the Moot Court Honor Society. He draws on two decades of experience in complex mediations, and from advanced trainings from the Syracuse University College of Law Mediation Assistance Program, negotiation and mediation training from the USDOJ National Advocacy Center, and from the Harvard Law School Program on Negotiation in conflict management and negotiations.

That range of experience is not incidental to this practice — it is the heart of this practice.

Understanding how employers build and defend discrimination cases makes him a better advocate for the people challenging them. Understanding how DOJ evaluates False Claims Act cases makes him a better advisor to the whistleblowers bringing them. Understanding how cases might resolve makes him a more honest counselor about when to fight and when to compromise.

Rob is proud to have spent the majority of his career helping employees and whistleblowers secure millions of dollars in damages for the unlawful conduct they've endured, and he looks forward to helping you too. Please feel free to reach out at any time. Thank you.

The Three-Sided View

Why having been on every side changes everything.

As a Plaintiff's Lawyer

Rob spent years representing workers in discrimination, retaliation, and wage cases. He understands what employees go through — the fear of losing their income, the uncertainty about whether anyone will believe them, and the power imbalance they face. That perspective never leaves.

As a Federal Prosecutor

As an AUSA, Rob evaluated cases through the lens of evidence, law, and institutional resources. He knows what the government finds compelling, how DOJ makes intervention decisions, and what separates a case that moves forward from one that doesn't. That institutional knowledge is rare in the private bar.

As a Defense Litigator

Rob has also defended employers. He knows every argument they make, every document they pull, and every strategy they use to minimize or defeat discrimination and whistleblower claims. That knowledge makes him a sharper advocate — because he has seen how the other side thinks.

Craft Mediation Northwest

Rob also mediates complex employment disputes.

In addition to this litigation practice, Rob is the founder of Craft Mediation Northwest, mediation services helping attorneys and their clients resolve complex employment, civil rights, and government employee matters. He has participated in approximately 300 mediations as an advocate or neutral, and that mediation experience directly informs how he evaluates and values cases as a litigator.
