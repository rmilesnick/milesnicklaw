Privacy & AI Use Notice

What this intake tool is, how it works, and what protections apply — and don't apply — at each stage.

1. What This Tool Is

The AI intake tool on this website is an automated screening system designed to collect limited, preliminary information to determine whether a consultation with Rob Milesnick may be appropriate. It is not a lawyer. It does not provide legal advice. It is not a substitute for an attorney-client relationship.

Under Oregon and Washington Rules of Professional Conduct, this tool functions as an automated nonlawyer assistant under the supervision of Rob Milesnick, who reviews qualifying submissions personally before any consultation is scheduled.

2. No Attorney-Client Relationship

Use of this tool does not create an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship with Milesnick Law is established only after: (a) a conflict of interest check has been completed; (b) a consultation has occurred; and (c) a written engagement agreement has been signed.

Until those conditions are met, you are a prospective client. Under RPC 1.18, Milesnick Law will not use or disclose information you provide through this tool to your detriment, and will not represent a party with materially adverse interests in the same matter without your consent — but full attorney-client privilege does not attach until a formal engagement is established.

3. What to Share — and What to Wait On

At the intake stage, general descriptions of your situation are appropriate and helpful. Highly detailed, sensitive, or privileged information should be reserved for a formal consultation, where full attorney-client protections apply.

In particular, at this stage please do not:

  • Share detailed documents or records from your employer's systems
  • Paste or upload sensitive communications or privileged materials
  • Provide information that you have not already shared with others or that you consider highly confidential

The intake tool is designed to assess whether your situation has the general characteristics of matters this practice handles — not to conduct a full legal intake.

4. How This Tool Works — AI Transparency

This intake system uses the Anthropic Claude API — a commercial AI service — to conduct conversational screening. The system operates as follows:

Your Input

Text you enter is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS connection to a Cloudflare Worker proxy, which forwards it to the Anthropic API for processing. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

AI Processing

The Anthropic API processes your input and generates a response. Under current Anthropic API terms, inputs and outputs are not used to train Anthropic's models. Cloudflare Workers process requests transiently and do not retain conversation data after the request is completed.

Attorney Review

When a conversation is routed for attorney review, a summary is submitted to Rob Milesnick via secure email. Access to submitted information is limited to Rob Milesnick and authorized staff.

Vendor terms are reviewed annually or upon notice of material changes. This disclosure was last verified May 2026.

5. Security Advisories

Do not use a work device or work email to access or communicate through this site. Employer IT systems may monitor communications on work devices and work email accounts. Use a personal device and personal email only.

Do not remove or copy documents from your employer's systems before speaking with an attorney. Doing so may violate your employment agreement, the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act, or both — and can affect your standing as a potential relator or plaintiff.

For False Claims Act matters: Do not discuss your concerns with journalists, on social media, or in public forums before consulting with an attorney. Public disclosure of potential fraud may affect your ability to bring a qui tam action as a relator.

6. Conflict Screening

Information submitted through this tool may be reviewed for purposes of screening for conflicts of interest. Providing information through this system may affect this firm's ability to represent other parties in the same or a related matter. If a conflict is identified, you will be informed and, where appropriate, referred to other resources.

7. Data Security

Information submitted through this system is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The system is hosted on infrastructure maintained by established commercial providers with documented security practices.

We do not represent that this system is absolutely secure. No system connected to the internet can provide that guarantee. We represent that reasonable, industry-standard security measures have been implemented consistent with our obligations under the Oregon and Washington Rules of Professional Conduct.

8. No Legal Advice — No Outcome Representations

Nothing generated by this tool — or by this website — constitutes legal advice. No statement made by this tool should be understood as an assessment of the strength of your legal claims, the likelihood of recovery, or the applicability of any law to your specific situation. Those determinations require attorney-client consultation and are the exclusive province of licensed counsel.

9. Jurisdictional Limitation

Rob Milesnick is licensed to practice law in the State of Oregon and the State of Washington, and is admitted to the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon, the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Washington, and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit. Legal services are provided only in jurisdictions where Rob Milesnick is licensed or where such representation is otherwise permitted by applicable rules.
